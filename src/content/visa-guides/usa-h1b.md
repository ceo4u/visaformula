# USA H-1B Work Visa Specialty Occupation Guide

The H-1B is a popular non-immigrant work visa that allows US employers to employ foreign workers in specialty occupations that require theoretical or technical expertise.

## Specialty Occupations
A specialty occupation requires the theoretical and practical application of a body of highly specialized knowledge, and a bachelor's degree or higher in the specific specialty as a minimum for entry into the occupation.

## Key Timelines
- **March**: H-1B lottery registration.
- **April**: Lottery results and petition filing window opens.
- **October 1st**: H-1B status becomes active.
