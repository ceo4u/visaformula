# Canada Study Permit Guide for Students

Canada remains one of the most welcoming destinations for international students. A study permit is a document issued by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) that allows foreign nationals to study at Designated Learning Institutions (DLIs) in Canada.

## Financial Proof (GIC Requirements)
Applicants must purchase a Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC) to prove they have sufficient funds. As of 2024, the minimum living expense proof is **CAD $20,635** for a single applicant.

## Step-by-Step Application Process
1. **Get acceptance letter from DLI**: Take 4-8 weeks.
2. **Arrange GIC & tuition**: Take 1-2 weeks.
3. **Apply online via IRCC portal**: Takes 1 day.
4. **Complete biometrics**: Take 1-2 weeks.
5. **Await decision**: Takes 4-16 weeks.
